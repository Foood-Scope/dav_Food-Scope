dav Food-Scope

FoodScope - a data-driven food discovery platform for Warangal that helps users explore restaurants, cafes, bakeries, and fast-food spots based on cuisine, price, ratings, ambience, and location. The platform organizes food establishments based on important factors such as location, cuisine, price range, ratings, ambience, and menu information, making it easier for users to compare options and decide where to eat.
