# 🍽️ FoodScope – Food Discovery Platform

### A Data-Driven Food Discovery Platform for Warangal

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**FoodScope** is a data-driven food discovery platform designed to help users explore restaurants, cafes, bakeries, and fast-food establishments across Warangal.

The platform organizes food establishments based on important factors such as **location, cuisine, price range, ratings, ambience, and menu information**, making it easier for users to compare options and make informed dining decisions.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔎 **Search & Explore:** Discover different food establishments.
- 📍 **Location-Based Filtering:** Find food places based on their location.
- 🍴 **Cuisine Categories:** Explore different cuisines and food categories.
- 💰 **Price Comparison:** Compare restaurant and menu price ranges.
- ⭐ **Ratings & Ambience:** Evaluate establishments based on customer ratings and ambience.
- 📊 **Data Analysis:** Analyze restaurant and menu-related information.
- 📈 **Interactive Dashboard:** Visualize collected data through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

The primary goal of FoodScope is to transform scattered restaurant information into a **simple, organized, and useful food-discovery platform**.

By collecting, cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing restaurant data, the project aims to help people discover suitable food options across Warangal based on their preferences and budget.

- Cafes

- Fast-food outlets

The collected dataset includes restaurant details, locations, cuisines, ratings, ambience, menu items, and pricing information.
